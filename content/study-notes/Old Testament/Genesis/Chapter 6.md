Genesis 6 shows us both how seriously God takes sin and how far His grace reaches — Noah found grace in the eyes of the LORD in the middle of a world that had completely given itself over to evil.

A few things worth noticing as you read:

- **Verses 1-4** describe just how far corruption had spread before the flood.
- **Verse 5** is God's own diagnosis: "every imagination of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually."
- **Verse 8** is the turning point of the whole chapter: "Noah found grace in the eyes of the LORD."

Judgment and grace sit right next to each other in this chapter. That tension runs through the whole Bible.
